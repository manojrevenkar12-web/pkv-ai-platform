# email-routing

_Generated by `scripts/design.py` from `config/usecases/routing.toml` and `reports/dev.json`. Do not edit by hand._

**Owner:** Customer Service. **Purpose:** Send every customer email to the team that will answer it.

## Model, chosen on the holdout weeks

| Candidate | Quality | Model cost per case | People and money paid wrongly per case | p95 latency | Verdict |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|---|
| classical | 99.39% | €0.0001 | €0.0257 | 1.4 s | clears the gate |
| small | 93.50% | €0.0008 | €0.2730 | 1.4 s | clears the gate |
| large | 95.78% | €0.0024 | €0.1773 | 4.5 s | cost 0.0024 over 0.002; p95 latency 4.5s over 3.0s |

Chosen: **classical**, the lowest cost per case all in among the candidates that clear the contract's gate
(quality at least 93%, model cost at most €0.002, p95 at most 3.0 s).

## Where it runs

**Lambda**: Lambda is cheaper until about 98,499 cases a day. Hosting at the contract's volume (180 cases a day):
Lambda €0.13 a month, two always-on Fargate tasks €69.26.
Inputs arrive in the SQS message.
Concurrency at peak: 1.

## Dependencies to settle before building

- Bedrock quota: 414 tokens a minute at peak, 0.2% of the account's default quota.
  No increase needed.
- Model access: `anthropic.claude-haiku-4-5`, `anthropic.claude-sonnet-4-5` enabled in the account; the use case's role may call no other model.

## Data

- model calls stay in eu-central-1: no cross-region inference profile outside the EU
- prompts and outputs are not logged in plain text; the audit log keeps ids and hashes
- inputs and outputs deleted after 90 days by an S3 lifecycle rule
