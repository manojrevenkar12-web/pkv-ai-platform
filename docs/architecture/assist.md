**Owner:** Concierge. **Purpose:** Draft an answer to a customer's chat question from the tariff conditions, for the agent to check and send.

## Model, chosen on the holdout weeks

| Candidate | Quality | Model cost per case | People and money paid wrongly per case | p95 latency | Verdict |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|---|
| small | 99.90% | €0.0036 | €0.9380 | 2.8 s | clears the gate |
| large | 99.80% | €0.0115 | €0.8330 | 9.0 s | p95 latency 9.0s over 6.0s |

Chosen: **small**: of the candidates that clear the contract's gate, the cheapest model, unless a dearer one saves
money per case all in by a margin a paired bootstrap over the holdout cases calls real at 90%
(quality at least 95%, model cost at most €0.02, p95 at most 6.0 s).

## Where it runs

**Lambda**: Lambda is cheaper until about 49,450 cases a day. Hosting at the contract's volume (100 cases a day):
Lambda €0.14 a month, two always-on Fargate tasks €69.26.
Inputs arrive in the SQS message.
Concurrency at peak: 1.

## Dependencies to settle before building

- Bedrock quota: 14,100 tokens a minute at peak, 7.0% of the account's default quota.
  No increase needed.
- Model access: `anthropic.claude-haiku-4-5`, `anthropic.claude-sonnet-4-5` enabled in the account; the use case's role may call no other model.

## Data

- model calls stay in eu-central-1: no cross-region inference profile outside the EU
- prompts and outputs are not logged in plain text; the audit log keeps ids and hashes
- health data: encrypted with a customer-managed KMS key, access only through the use case's own role
- inputs and outputs deleted after 365 days by an S3 lifecycle rule
