# invoice-check

_Generated by `scripts/design.py` from `config/usecases/invoices.toml` and `reports/dev.json`. Do not edit by hand._

**Owner:** Claims. **Purpose:** Read a doctor's invoice, check it against the fee schedule and the tariff, and settle it or send it to a person.

## Model, chosen on the holdout weeks

| Candidate | Quality | Model cost per case | People and money paid wrongly per case | p95 latency | Verdict |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|---|
| small | 99.86% | €0.0082 | €0.0560 | 22.8 s | clears the gate |
| large | 99.64% | €0.0212 | €0.1389 | 29.0 s | clears the gate |

Chosen: **small**: of the candidates that clear the contract's gate, the cheapest model, unless a dearer one saves
money per case all in by a margin a paired bootstrap over the holdout cases calls real at 90%
(quality at least 97%, model cost at most €0.03, p95 at most 60.0 s).

## Where it runs

**Lambda**: Lambda is cheaper until about 2,383 cases a day. Hosting at the contract's volume (140 cases a day):
Lambda €4.88 a month, two always-on Fargate tasks €83.09.
Inputs above Lambda's 6 MB synchronous payload arrive through S3 and an SQS message carries the key.
Concurrency at peak: 4.

## Dependencies to settle before building

- Bedrock quota: 27,695 tokens a minute at peak, 13.8% of the account's default quota.
  No increase needed.
- Model access: `anthropic.claude-haiku-4-5`, `anthropic.claude-sonnet-4-5` enabled in the account; the use case's role may call no other model.

## Data

- model calls stay in eu-central-1: no cross-region inference profile outside the EU
- prompts and outputs are not logged in plain text; the audit log keeps ids and hashes
- health data: encrypted with a customer-managed KMS key, access only through the use case's own role
- inputs and outputs deleted after 3650 days by an S3 lifecycle rule
